# PoleSafe 🚸 — Video Script 1: A Day with PoleSafe

## Setting
Talking avatar, friendly and excited. Imagine you're explaining to a friend over a cup of coffee.

---

**[SCENE 1 — THE PROBLEM]**

"Hey, let me tell you about this thing I'm building. You know how every morning in Kampala, parents are rushing to get their kids to school? And the biggest worry isn't traffic — it's 'Will my child actually get to school safely?'

We've all heard the stories. A driver picks up the wrong kid. A boda rider nobody knows shows up instead of the regular driver. A parent drops their child at school and has no idea if they actually arrived.

And for the drivers — they're doing everything by phone calls and WhatsApp groups. No proper system to show they're reliable. No way for parents to choose them because of their good record.

That's why I built PoleSafe."

**[SCENE 2 — PARENT BOOKS A RIDE]**

"So here's how it works. Imagine you're a parent in Ntinda. Your child goes to school in Bukoto. Every morning, you open PoleSafe on your phone.

The night before, you book your child's ride. You pick the time — let's say 6:30 AM pickup. You pick your child's name. And you choose your vehicle type — maybe you want a car today because it's raining, or a boda because it's faster in traffic.

One tap. The ride request goes out."

**[SCENE 3 — FINDING THE RIGHT DRIVER]**

"Now here's where it gets interesting. PoleSafe doesn't just grab any random driver. The system looks for the best driver for YOUR child.

It checks: Who's available nearby right now? Who has the best rating? Does this child have a favorite driver they always ride with? Is the driver properly approved and vetted?

And if you're booking a bigger vehicle — a taxi or a bus — the system sends your request to multiple drivers. They each quote their price. You see all the quotes and pick the one that works for you. It's like Uber, but designed for school children."

**[SCENE 4 — MORNING PICKUP]**

"Morning comes. 6:30 AM. The driver arrives at your gate.

But before the child gets in, there's something special. Every ride has a unique pickup code — like a password. The parent tells the child the code. When the driver arrives, the child says the code. If the code matches, everyone knows this is the RIGHT driver.

The driver then takes a photo of the child getting into the vehicle. Not a selfie — a real photo proving 'I picked up this child at this time.' This is evidence. If anything ever goes wrong, we have proof.

The driver also takes their own selfie at the start of the ride. So no one can impersonate a PoleSafe driver."

**[SCENE 5 — THE JOURNEY TO SCHOOL]**

"Now the child is in the vehicle. The parent can see exactly where the vehicle is on a live map. Not just 'the driver is on the way' — real-time GPS tracking.

The parent watches the little car icon moving from their home towards the school. They can see the speed. They can see the route. They know their child is safe.

And the driver? They're following the best route calculated by the system — avoiding the worst Kampala traffic."

**[SCENE 6 — ARRIVING AT SCHOOL]**

"The vehicle arrives at the school gate. The school gate guard checks the child in — this is the gate confirmation. The school now knows 'Child X has arrived safely.'

The parent gets a notification: 'Your child has arrived at school.' Peace of mind. No more calling the driver asking 'Have you reached?'

And here's a really cool safety feature — for the school trips, the driver doesn't even see the child's full name until they actually ARRIVE at the school. Before that, the child appears as 'Child #3A1F' — a code. So even if someone steals the driver's phone, they can't know which children belong to which family.

Once the driver taps 'Arrived at School,' the names are revealed. That's when they know exactly who they're picking up."

**[SCENE 7 — DURING THE SCHOOL DAY]**

"During the day, the child is safely at school. But what if the child gets sick?

The parent opens PoleSafe, taps 'Sick Day,' and gets a credit for that ride. No wasted money. The credit goes back to their account for a future ride.

What if the parent needs to pick up the child early? They can request an early pickup. The nearest available driver gets notified and heads to the school.

And the school? They use PoleSafe too. Teachers can verify the driver at pickup — a second layer of safety. The school can send broadcasts to all parents. They can mark detention kids who shouldn't be picked up yet."

**[SCENE 8 — AFTERNOON PICKUP]"

"School ends. Time to go home.

The parent doesn't need to book again if they have a regular subscription — it's a weekly or monthly plan. The system already knows the child needs to go home.

A driver is matched. They head to the school. The teacher verifies the driver. The child gets into the vehicle. Another pickup photo is taken.

The parent watches the live map as their child travels back home.

The vehicle arrives. The child is home safe. The parent gets a notification: 'Your child has arrived home.'"

**[SCENE 9 — THE PAYMENT]"

"At the end of the week or month, the parent gets their statement. Payments are handled through Mobile Money or card — Flutterwave handles it all.

Breakdown: How many rides? Any sick day credits? Fuel adjustment this week? Everything is clear. No guessing.

The driver gets paid too. Their earnings show exactly: school rides, ride-hailing rides, total payouts. PoleSafe takes a small commission — 5% — to keep the system running."

**[SCENE 10 — WHY IT MATTERS]"

"So that's PoleSafe. From booking in the evening to safe arrival at school in the morning. From live tracking during the day to a secure pickup back home in the afternoon.

Every step has safety built in: pickup codes, teacher verification, photo evidence, phone number masking, driver selfies.

Every step has transparency: live GPS tracking, notifications at every stage, clear payments.

And every step considers the driver too: fair pay, ratings to build their reputation, a proper system to prove they're reliable.

It's not just a ride-hailing app for kids. It's a complete school transport safety system built for Uganda.

🚸"
